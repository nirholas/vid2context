# Getting help

- **Questions, setup help, "how do I...":** ask in [Discussions → Q&A](https://github.com/nirholas/vid2context/discussions/categories/q-a).
- **Ideas and feature requests:** start in [Discussions → Ideas](https://github.com/nirholas/vid2context/discussions/categories/ideas).
- **Bugs:** [open a bug report](https://github.com/nirholas/vid2context/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Include the `attempts` list from the result or error; it shows which providers were tried and why each one failed.
- **New platform or AI service:** [request a provider](https://github.com/nirholas/vid2context/issues/new?template=provider_request.yml).
- **Security problems:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and report privately.
- **Built something with vid2context?** Share it in [Show and tell](https://github.com/nirholas/vid2context/discussions/categories/show-and-tell).

The [llms-full.txt](llms-full.txt) guide covers configuration and every environment variable. You can also paste it into your AI assistant and ask it questions.
